Hosting and server requests

This website is published using GitHub Pages, a hosting service provided by GitHub, Inc. When you visit the site, GitHub processes technical connection data needed to deliver it. This may include your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, the referring page, browser and device information, and the HTTP status.

This processing supports delivery, stability, and security of the site. Its legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure and reliable website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub processes this information as the hosting provider under its own privacy terms. For details about GitHub’s processing and retention, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Externally hosted fonts

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser connects to Google servers to retrieve the font files. This may transmit your IP address, browser information, and the page you are visiting to Google.

We use the fonts based on our legitimate interest in a consistent, readable presentation (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy. Depending on the connection and providers involved, processing outside the European Economic Area cannot be ruled out; Google describes applicable safeguards in its privacy information.

Contact by email

This site includes an email link. If you choose to send us a message, we process the information you provide, including your email address and message, to respond. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in handling enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR); where your enquiry is about entering into a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR also applies. Your email provider and our email service will also process the message.

We retain the message only as long as needed to handle your enquiry, unless statutory retention obligations apply.
